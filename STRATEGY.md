# Evoview component library — purpose and agent brief

## Business model

Evoview offers selected businesses with smaller marketing budgets premium
websites at discounted prices. The intended value of the work exceeds the
discounted project fee; the commercial rationale is not to recover all of that
value from the initial website sale alone.

Each participating client site also becomes a lasting demonstration of Evoview's
craft and a referral surface. Distinctive components, coherent site structure,
shared design principles and subtle Evoview signatures build recognition across
a network of otherwise individually branded businesses. Relevant attribution
links let interested visitors move from a client's website into Evoview's own
website and enquiry funnel.

The intended cycle is: deliver a valuable client experience → expose visitors
to recognizable Evoview work → earn qualified interest and enquiries → grow the
client network → create further exposure. The client's contribution is therefore
both the agreed fee and the ongoing visibility generated by its site. This is a
long-term acquisition strategy, not an instruction to lower quality for clients
with smaller budgets.

The founder reports strong conversion results from an existing client's custom
reservation system. Treat this as founder-reported context, not audited metrics
or proof of future referral growth. Cumulative profitability and brand prestige
are strategic goals, not guaranteed outcomes.

## What this means for agents

- **Serve the client first.** The site must help its visitors understand the
  business and complete its main actions, such as reservations. Evoview publicity
  must complement that experience, not interrupt it or replace client calls to
  action.
- **Preserve recognizable authorship.** Keep approved signature choreography,
  interaction quality, composition and subtle branding. Do not strip these away
  as incidental decoration when extracting or adapting a component.
- **Adapt without making clones.** Separate client content, media, palette,
  typography and actions from reusable behavior. Build coherence through shared
  design principles, not identical pages or indiscriminate logo repetition.
- **Make attribution intentional.** Document the approved Evoview mark, its
  placement and its explicit link destination. Make links understandable and
  accessible; do not turn unrelated client interactions into surprise referrals.
  Use a supplied or verified destination, never invent one. Do not add tracking,
  redirects or new branding placements without authorization.
- **Package the complete experience.** Include desktop/mobile states, sequence
  timing, hover/touch/keyboard behavior, media requirements, loading and pause
  rules, reduced-motion behavior, and a visual/interaction verification checklist.
  Record source behavior accurately; flag gaps instead of silently redesigning it.
- **Carry the rationale forward.** Include these relevant obligations in each
  component's README, agent skill and client handoff. Specify what can change
  freely and what constitutes the protected Evoview signature. A future agent
  should not need the founder to repeat the business brief.
- **Evaluate both roles.** Check client utility and the visibility/clarity of the
  approved Evoview attribution. Do not claim acquisition or conversion results
  without evidence, and do not treat this strategy as authorization to change
  pricing, client agreements or live sites.

## Reference: Tchin Tchin karaoke video cards

The project titled `test` lives at `/root/.vibehub/projects/test`. Its karaoke
page contains the four-card `VideoGallery` under “Vivez l'Expérience”. Relevant
sources are `src/components/karaoke/VideoGallery.tsx`, its CSS sidecar,
`src/components/media/MediaWatermark.tsx` and `src/data/karaoke-data.ts`.

This is a reference for the strategy, not yet a packaged library component:

- Four video cards carry Evoview watermarks.
- The automatic focus sequence has a 2,000 ms dwell per card.
- Desktop mouse hover selects a card and suspends the automatic clock; leaving
  resumes the prior sequence with its remaining dwell. Adjacent-card hover
  handoffs keep the clock suspended.
- Visibility and reduced-motion conditions gate the automatic sequence; the
  source also contains media playback and pointer-tilt behavior to inventory
  before extraction.
- **Current implementation is not the complete referral model:** card links
  lead to the restaurant's Instagram, and the watermark is decorative, not a
  separate Evoview link. Preserve that distinction in reports. Adding an Evoview
  referral interaction requires an explicit implementation scope; this brief
  does not silently change existing destinations.

On 2026-10-18, the running dev service was inspected at
`http://localhost:4888/karaoke/`: four watermarks were present and hovering the
third card held it active beyond the normal dwell. The public project URL
`https://test.said.bet/karaoke` returned a blank browser page during this check.
This was a targeted reference inspection, not a full responsive/accessibility QA.
